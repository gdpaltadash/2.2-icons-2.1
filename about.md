# Icons 2.2 for GD 2.113

Adds the icons (cube, ship, ball, UFO, wave, robot, spider) and the 107 colors of
Geometry Dash 2.2 to 2.113, with a new garage in the style of 2.2.

## Install

1. Copy the `icons` folder of GD 2.2 (`Resources\icons`) into the `Resources`
   folder of GD 2.113.
2. Copy `local.icons22_2113.geode` into `geode\mods`.

Everything is unlocked. Swing, fire effects and other 2.2-only content are not included.
